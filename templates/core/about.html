{% trans "Campus Gabaa: Problem, Architecture & Vision" %}

{% trans "Solving information asymmetry and price fragmentation across Haramaya University and surrounding retail markets using open-source, zero-budget cloud engineering." %}

1. {% trans "The Real-World Campus Problem" %}

{% trans "Haramaya University (HU) is one of Ethiopia's premier higher education institutions, hosting over 30,000 undergraduate and postgraduate students. However, the campus retail geography is intensely fragmented across distinct geographical clusters:" %}

{% trans "Main Campus Kiosks" %}

{% trans "Scattered near student dorm blocks (Blocks 1-60) and central administration. Highly convenient, but frequently apply heavy markups and experience rapid stockouts." %}

{% trans "Bate Gate & Town Market" %}

{% trans "The bustling commercial street immediately outside HU campus gates. Offers competitive pricing on stationery, electronics, and groceries, but requires physical travel and price inquiry." %}

{% trans "Prior to Campus Gabaa, students faced three major inefficiencies: (a) severe price variance for identical items (e.g. 100-page notebook varying from 85 to 130 ETB across 3 kiosks), (b) wasted physical walking time searching for items out of stock, and (c) inability to verify whether price quotes heard from peers are still current." %}

2. {% trans "The Campus Gabaa Solution" %}

{% trans "Campus Gabaa (GabaaHU) is a decentralized campus shopping directory and price-comparison engine designed specifically for low-bandwidth mobile connections and multilingual Ethiopian communities." %}

  • {% trans "Canonical Product Catalog Decoupled from Shops" %}

    {% trans "Instead of duplicate listings that clutter search results, a single product record (e.g., 'Deli 0.5mm Gel Pen') aggregates and ranks all competing shop offers by price." %}

  • {% trans "Automated Stale-Price Indicators" %}

    {% trans "In an inflationary economy, price freshness is essential. Listings track verification timestamps and are visually categorized as Fresh (< 3 days), Expiring (3-7 days), or Stale (> 7 days)." %}

  • {% trans "Crowd-Sourced Accuracy Reporting & Moderation" %}

    {% trans "Students report price discrepancies or closed shops directly from product pages, empowering campus moderators to keep the data trustworthy." %}

  • {% trans "Trilingual Localization (Afaan Oromoo, Amharic, English)" %}

    {% trans "Deep internationalization using GNU gettext, offering seamless native-language navigation for the East Hararghe region." %}
